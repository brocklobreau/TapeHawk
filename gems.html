How the score works

Gates first. Out if trailing net margin is zero or negative, free cash flow is negative, revenue is shrinking, debt is over twice equity, or there are no earnings to price. A story stock can't score its way in.

Value, up to 30. P/E under 12 earns 12 points, under 18 earns 8, under 25 earns 5 only if growth is over 15%. EV/EBITDA under 10 earns 8, under 15 earns 4. Free-cash-flow yield (cash generated per share over the price) of 6%+ earns 10, 3%+ earns 5.

Growth, up to 30. Revenue growth of 20%+ earns 15, 10%+ earns 10, 5%+ earns 5. Earnings-per-share growth of 20%+ earns 15, 10%+ earns 8.

Quality, up to 30. Net margin 15%+ earns 10, 8%+ earns 6, positive earns 3. Return on equity 15%+ earns 8, 10%+ earns 4. Debt under half of equity earns 7, under equity earns 4. Current ratio of 1.5+ earns 5.

Hidden, up to 10. Under $2B earns 4, under $10B earns 2. Trading in the bottom 30% of its 52-week range earns 3. Analyst consensus target 20%+ above the price earns 3.

Reading it. 70+ means all three tests passed at once — rare. 55+ means two clearly did. 40+ is one clear strength. Everything else passed the gates and not much more. Numbers come from the latest reported annual figures and the current price, so a company that just reported will move the next day.

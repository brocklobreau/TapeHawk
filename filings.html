Currently tracking Schedule 13D and Form 8-K.

A Schedule 13D means somebody crossed 5% of a company and did not claim to be passive about it — they may push for board seats, a sale, or a change of strategy. Since the SEC's 2023 amendments took effect the filing is due within 5 business days of crossing the threshold, and amendments within 2, so it now describes something current rather than something from a fortnight ago.

This is a disclosure feed, not a recommendation. A 13D says a large holder showed up; it does not say the stock goes up, and an amendment can just as easily be that holder trimming. Everything here is pulled straight from EDGAR and links back to the filing itself — read the primary document before you act on any row.

Material events Form 8-K

Companies file hundreds of 8-Ks a day and nearly all of them are procedural. This section carries the ones that are not. An 8-K is only a container — the item number says what happened — so each row is tagged red flag or tailwind from the item and, where the number alone can't settle it, from the filing's own words. Item 5.02 covers both a CFO resigning and a company hiring one, so that row is read rather than assumed; an item 8.01 or 1.01 is listed only when the document says something specific. Direction here is what the filing is, not a prediction of where the stock goes. A row marked no wire coverage means no headline mentioning that ticker reached this site in the window around the filing — an observation about our own archive, not proof nobody noticed.

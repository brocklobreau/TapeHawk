Live Halts SEC Filings Hidden Gems Scoreboard

Does the Big News rail actually flag the stories that move stocks? Every headline gets checked against what its stock actually did in the hour afterwards, and this page is the answer — including the calls that were wrong.

Ordinary headlines are graded too, deliberately: they are the control. A Big News median of 7% is only impressive next to what a routine headline did. Read the sample size next to every number. A hit rate over five headlines is not a hit rate. Moves are measured from the open of the first 5-minute bar at or after the headline, so the reaction is not counted before it could have been acted on. Headlines arriving outside market hours can't be measured and are excluded rather than counted as zero.
